# M.O.M. Family Ops Module

Family Ops is the shared handoff board for the everyday tasks that usually live in one caregiver's head.

## Purpose

Dose & Go answers: what did we give?
Fever Log answers: what happened?
Appointments answers: what do we need to ask or do next?
Emergency Card answers: what matters in a crisis?
Family Ops answers: who needs what today, and who owns it?

## MVP behaviors

- Add a task for a specific child or the whole family.
- Assign an owner.
- Categorize the work as morning routine, school, health, home, errand, meal, admin, or other.
- Set priority and optional due date/time.
- Add checklist items and blocker notes.
- Filter by child/scope, status, and owner.
- Mark done, reopen, move to today, delete, or clear.
- Copy a handoff summary for messaging another caregiver.

## Safety and scope

Family Ops is a coordination layer. It does not replace emergency services, clinical guidance, school systems, calendars, legal reminders, or professional care. It is designed to make handoffs clearer and reduce mental load.

## Portfolio angle

This module expands M.O.M. from health-specific logging into a broader parent operations system. It shows information architecture, local persistence, cross-module continuity, and product judgment around what should remain lightweight.
