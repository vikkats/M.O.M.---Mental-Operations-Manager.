# Emergency Card module

Emergency Card is the fourth active M.O.M. module after Dose & Go, Fever Log, and Appointments.

## Product role

The module turns caregiver-entered emergency details into a copy-ready handoff card. It is designed for moments when a parent, babysitter, school, relative, clinician, or emergency service needs concise context fast.

## MVP capabilities

- Create one emergency card per child profile.
- Store allergies and reactions.
- Store important medical conditions and current medication notes.
- Store pediatrician/doctor details.
- Store preferred hospital or clinic.
- Store insurance provider and policy details.
- Store two emergency contacts.
- Store access, language, custody, or handoff notes.
- Copy one card.
- Copy all filtered cards.
- Open a print view for a single card.
- Edit/delete individual cards.
- Clear all emergency card entries.

## Safety and privacy framing

Emergency Card is a caregiver-entered record only. It does not verify medical facts, recommend treatment, replace emergency services, or replace official medical records.

Because this is a public portfolio prototype, real sensitive data should not be entered into the hosted demo. The production version would need authentication, encryption, audit history, emergency access rules, consent boundaries, and privacy review.

## UX rationale

The module is intentionally formatted as a wallet card instead of a long document. In a stressful moment, users need scannable information:

1. Child identity and current weight.
2. Allergies and reactions.
3. Conditions and medication context.
4. Doctor and hospital details.
5. Emergency contacts.
6. Insurance/access notes.

The copy/print actions make the module useful beyond the app screen, especially for school forms, babysitter handoff, family coordination, and clinician conversations.
